Midnight Manor

Escape the haunted manor before your sanity collapses. Search each wing, collect clues, power your flashlight, and unlock the final gate.

Controls

  • Move: Arrow keys / WASD or touch controls
  • Interact: E or Interact button
  • Flashlight: F or Light button

Sanity: 100

Battery: 100%

Inventory: Empty

Location: Foyer

Objective: Find a way out.
